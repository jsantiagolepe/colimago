# Surface brief: public landing (`/`, `public/index.html`)

Scope: the public landing of colimago.mx and the `/login` modal it hosts. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.
Audience: people living in or visiting Colima; action: install Colima Go (App Store / Google Play). Proof: the app's real photos, municipality illustrations and copy (see PRODUCT.md Evidence). Constraints: static single file, no dependencies, `/login` Google modal preserved and unadvertised, no dated events, light/dark with system preference + persistence, reduced motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Colima Go is a walk down a Colima fair street at night: the string of bulbs of the feria leads visitors from district to district, and each district is a real part of Colima (Playas, Rincones Naturales, Fiestas del Pueblo, Sabores, Municipios). It refuses the category default of a sunny full-bleed beach hero over a grid of destination cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night ground in wet-asphalt indigo-black (#0e1119), light-bulb saffron (#ffc347) for light and the primary action, lona blue (#1e3a6e) and banner red (#c8322b) as awning and sign inks, steam white (#f2f1ee) text. Hand-painted rótulo lettering (Shrikhand) for signs only, Barlow Condensed for board labels, Barlow for reading. Components are hanging sign boards (each district's title is lettered on its own board in lona blue, banner red or bulb saffron, hung from its wire), brush-stroke painted buttons, and bulbs on a catenary wire. Photos are the stalls. By day (light theme) the ground is whitewashed cal (#f4f4f1), the bulbs are unlit glass, and the awning inks carry the color.

STORY: The visitor sees Colima's real places and fiestas, believes the whole state fits in one app made from real local content, and taps a store button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The night ground with a bulb wire sagging across the top; the huge painted H1 "Colima en la palma de tu mano" on the left at about 7vw; beneath it one line of real copy and the two saffron painted store buttons (App Store, Google Play), which are the primary action per the confirmed answer that downloading the app is the one action (no separate "Descarga gratis" button); on the right, five large photo sign boards hang from that same wire in two staggered rows filling the column (Playas, Rincones Naturales, Fiestas del Pueblo, Sabores, Municipios), each linking to its district. On phones the boards become a swipeable row under the CTA.

FORM: Night-market sign river (catalog challenger, chosen by the user over my list's #6 "Arena volcánica"; seed key 3a83cb91), translated to a Colima feria under the brief's minimalist-premium pin.

SIGNATURE: The wire of bulbs is the page's thread: each district's sign lights its bulbs when it enters the viewport (one orchestrated scroll reveal), and the hero boards swing on hover or focus.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
