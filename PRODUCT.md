# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Visitors to colimago.mx** (public landing, `/`): people who live in or are planning to visit the state of Colima, México, deciding whether the Colima Go app is worth installing. Spanish-speaking first.
- **People using the web guide** (`/inicio`, `/explorar`, `/calendario`, `/favoritos`, `/ajustes` and the place, municipality, festival and event pages; also under `/en/`): colimenses and visitors who arrive from a shared Facebook/WhatsApp link, browse what is on and where to go, save favorites, and may sign in with Google to like and comment. Spanish first, English second.
- **The Colima Go administrator** (`/login` → `/dashboard.html`): a single Google account that reviews events detected from municipal Facebook pages, publishes them to the app, and maintains local places, the places directory and the Facebook pages directory.

## Product Purpose

Colima Go is a free mobile app (iOS and Android), described by its own copy as a social network for discovering and sharing the places and events of Colima. This repo (`colimago`) serves its public landing page, the web guide (the web counterpart of the app: same five tabs, same data) and the private admin panel. The landing succeeds when a visitor installs the app from the App Store or Google Play. The guide succeeds when someone finds the event or place they were sent, keeps browsing, and eventually installs the app.

## Positioning

Colima Go covers only Colima: its ten municipalities, its natural spots and its town festivals. Its event feed is built from what the municipal governments themselves publish, which the administrator reviews before it goes live.

## Operating Context

- The app's main sections (from the ColimaApp repo, as the code has them): Inicio (a hub: map of the ten municipalities, Top 10, access to Fiestas Charrotaurinas, **Fiestas del Pueblo**, **Rincones Naturales** — nine categories —, Playas, **Municipios**, Tianguis y mercados), Explorar (the social feed: events mixed with places), Calendario, Favoritos, Ajustes. The guide mirrors them.
- Event types used by the panel: Deportivo, Social, Musical, Cultural, Ganadero, Religioso, Gastronómico, Otro.
- Local catalog categories: Ríos, Playas, Lagos/Lagunas, Montañas, Hoteles, Restaurantes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Express server (`server.js`) deployed on Vercel; `public/` is static, `views/dashboard.html` is session-gated. No build step, no front-end framework; the landing is a single static HTML file and must stay dependency-free, with one deliberate exception: the async AdSense snippet in `<head>`, which Google needs to verify the site (the landing itself has no ad slots). The guide (`lib/guia/`) is server-rendered HTML (template functions, vanilla JS in `public/js/`, CSS in `public/css/`), also without a build step; see `docs/guia-web.md`.
- `/login` opens the Google sign-in modal on the landing page; the login is intentionally not advertised on the public page. This behavior and the Google client id must be preserved.
- Since October 2026 the admin panel is switched off (`PANEL_ACTIVO` in `server.js`): `/login`, `/dashboard.html`, `/auth/*` and `/api/*` return 404. Its code, including the login modal, is kept on purpose for a future reactivation; see "Panel de administración: desactivado" in the README.
- The landing's content is static: it must not show dated events that can go stale. The guide does show dated events: they come from the backend's public `GET /post/` (read through this server, cached for a minute, never directly from the browser), and the guide separates upcoming from past events instead of letting them go stale.
- Places, municipalities, town festivals and the Feria come from data exported from the app (`data/`, generated by `scripts/importar-app-movil.js`), not from the backend, which does not serve the places catalog publicly.
- Likes, comments, account favorites, reporting and hiding need Google sign-in (platform `web`); the session is an encrypted httpOnly cookie, never a token in the page. Favorites also work without an account (in the browser).
- The landing's intro section and its footer link to the guide and to the information pages (`/privacidad`, `/terminos`, `/contacto`, `/acerca-de`), which live on the same domain (AdSense requires the privacy policy there). The counts it quotes (rincones, tianguis y mercados, fiestas, playas) are written by hand in `public/index.html`; `npm run verificar:sitio` checks them against `data/`. The store buttons are secondary on purpose: the guide is the primary action.
- Indexing: only pages good enough to be judged are indexed (English pages only when their main text is English, no past events, no sample hotels or restaurants); see `lib/guia/indexacion.js` and "Qué se indexa" in `docs/guia-web.md`.
- Ads: the app uses AdMob; the web uses Google AdSense when `ADSENSE_CLIENT` and the slot variables are set, and a house promo in the same reserved slot otherwise.

## Product Principles

1. Colima is the protagonist; the app is the way in.
2. Only what is real: real places, real photos, real municipal content.
3. Everything the landing shows has to exist in the app; the guide shows what the app shows, in the same words.
4. The admin path stays out of sight on the public page.
5. Ads pay for the app and are respected: reserved space, never over content, never on empty, error or form states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public page: WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, and support for reduced-motion preferences.
